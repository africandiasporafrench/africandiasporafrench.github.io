***ADFC is a free, digital community class for Black and Indigenous folks to world-build through skill-share. We practice French in our praxis to become global citizens, and we learn African and African diasporic history that ties to the American Gulf South to demystify the past and imagine better futures!*** 

This course is geared towards adult learners with interest in the French language and African history. The missions of the class are to create a network of cool Black and Indigenous global citizens from all over, world-build by creating free access to education, and getting bi-lingual by learning *how* to learn languages. *We have been around since 2023 and don’t plan on slowin’ down!*

The curriculum of this class somewhat mimics that of a university course but the pace will not be too fast. Class is Saturday & Sunday morning (with Sunday morning sessions recorded and uploaded here on the site for attendees and practitioners of religious and spiritual services🙏🏿).

## Q & A:

- Is it too late to join the class? No! Just catch up with the recordings below and join anytime (Zoom link in syllabus)
- Do I have to do the homework? Completely up to you. You get as much from this class as you put in. If you send me any work, I will check it!
- What is the goal for the course? (1) Connect with community, (2) Boosting confidence in speaking, (3) Practice and learn a new language, (4) Open eyes to the French-speaking side of the African Diaspora and (5) Eventually see ourselves as a part of that Afro-Francophonie 💞 **We are citizens of the world!**
